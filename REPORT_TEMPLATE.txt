3. INTRODUCTION
   The Smart Pantry Manager solves the problem of food waste by letting users track leftover ingredients at home and suggesting only recipes they can cook with what they already have. No shopping list, no almost-matches – strict matching only. Target users: students, busy households, anyone who wants to use leftovers.

7. CHALLENGES AND SOLUTIONS
   1. Preventing ANR: used pure local SQLite, no network, small data set, fast queries.
   2. Singular/plural ingredient names: simple normalise() method instead of exact string match.
   3. Showing empty state cleanly: visibility toggle between RecyclerView and message TextView.
